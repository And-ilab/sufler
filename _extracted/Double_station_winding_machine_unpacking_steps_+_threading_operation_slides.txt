How to fix the stator
1. In the "step" state, click "Clamp open" on the screen, put in the stator, adjust the position, and click "Clamp open" again. The tooling enters the "close" clamping state and the stator is locked.
2. In the "step" state, after all steps are completed, the touch screen switches from "MAN." to "AUTO", and the operating table rotation button also turns to "auto". At this time, all preparations are completed.
